Meta Ads: 5,235 Real Estate Leads

A real case study based on 120 campaigns run for Home Stories Ltd.

Most real estate campaigns fail because they collect hundreds of useless numbers from people who cannot afford the property. When I took over the ad account for Home Stories Ltd, my focus was to filter for high-intent buyers in Dhaka before they ever submitted a form.

5,235
Verified buyer leads delivered
$0.80 - $2.00
Filtered lead acquisition cost
64%
Sales team connection rate

How I structured the campaigns:

1. Pre-qualifying Instant Forms: Instead of simple one-click forms, I added mandatory questions on budget (1.5 Cr+ BDT), purchase timeframe, and verified phone numbers. This cut out casual browsers and saved the sales team hours of follow-up calls.

2. Geographic targeting: Focused ad spend specifically on affluent zones in Dhaka: Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara, Dhanmondi, and Uttara. Excluded generic regions that wasted budget.

3. Full attribution tracking: Set up Meta Conversions API (CAPI) on the website alongside the Pixel to make sure iOS users and ad-blockers did not break the tracking data.

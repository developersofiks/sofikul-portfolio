Verified Performance Marketing Case Study

Direct execution benchmark for premium residential & commercial real estate lead generation (Home Stories Ltd data).

2-Tier Lead Qualification

Rather than collecting junk leads, implemented mandatory qualification questions in Meta Instant Forms (Budget > 1.5 Cr BDT, Purchase Timeline < 6 Months, Real Phone OTP check).

Result: Sales team connection rate increased from 18% to 64%.

Micro-Affluence Audiences

Precision targeting focused on High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNWI) in Gulshan, Banani, Baridhara, Dhanmondi, and Uttara. Excluded generic bargain seekers to preserve CBO sales time.

Result: $0.80 - $2.00 (100 - 250 BDT) CPL for verified buyers.

Full-Funnel Attribution

Integrated Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and Google Tag Manager (GTM) to bridge the offline CRM gap—feeding qualified sales pipeline data back into Meta algorithm for lookalike modeling.

Result: 5,235 leads delivered across 120 campaigns.
